### Life Really Sucks Sometimes

Life sucks in ways words can't explain,\
You smile in public, go home with pain.\
You laugh with people, pretend you're okay,\
Then fight your own thoughts at the end of the day.

You give your best, still lose your place,\
Keep wiping tears no one sees on your face.\
You love too hard, get broken instead,\
With a thousand things screaming inside your head.

Bills keep coming, dreams fall apart,\
People leave holes right inside your heart.\
You try to be strong, but you're tired of trying,\
Tired of pretending, tired of crying.

And the worst part is, the world doesn't pause,\
Nobody asks why, nobody knows.\
You wake up tomorrow and do it again,\
Same empty feelings, same invisible pain.

Maybe life isn't fair, maybe it never will be,\
Maybe we're all just trying to feel free.\
So here's to the nights when nothing feels right,\
To the silent battles we fight out of sight.

Because sometimes life doesn't need a lesson or a reason. Sometimes it just sucks, and you're allowed to admit it.
